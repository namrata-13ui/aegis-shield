# Incident Response Policy

**Owner:** Incident Lead
**Review Date:** 22 April 2027
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Ensure security incidents are reported and handled consistently.

## Scope
All users, systems and approved service providers.

## Rules
1. Users must report suspected incidents immediately and within one hour of discovery.
2. Each confirmed incident must have a record, severity and assigned owner.
3. Responders must preserve relevant evidence where safe and practical.
4. Containment actions must be recorded with timestamps.
5. The incident lead must document impact, actions and lessons learned.
6. High-severity incidents must receive a post-incident review within 10 business days.
7. Corrective actions must have named owners and due dates.

## Roles
Users report incidents; responders investigate; the incident lead coordinates response.

## Exceptions
Any deviation must be documented in the incident record.

## Review
Review every six months and after a major incident.
