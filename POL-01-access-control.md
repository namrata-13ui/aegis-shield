# Access Control Policy

**Owner:** Security Lead
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Review Date:** 22 April 2027
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Ensure users access only the systems and data required for their work.

## Scope
All users, administrators, accounts and systems in Aura Workforce / Aegis Shield.

## Rules
1. Every user must have a unique account.
2. Access must be approved by the system owner before it is granted.
3. Users must receive only the permissions required for their role.
4. Administrator access must be reviewed every 90 days.
5. Access must be disabled when a user's authorised access ends.

## Roles
Security Lead reviews the policy; system owners approve access; IT manages accounts; users protect their accounts.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented justification, approval and an expiry date.

## Review
Review every six months.
