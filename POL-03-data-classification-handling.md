# Data Classification and Handling Policy

**Owner:** Data Owner
**Review Date:** 22 April 2027
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Protect data according to its sensitivity.

## Scope
All data created, processed, stored or shared by the organisation.

## Rules
1. Every dataset must have an owner and a recorded classification.
2. Data must be classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
3. Confidential and Restricted data must not be placed in public repositories.
4. Access must be limited to authorised people with a business need.
5. Test data must be synthetic or de-identified unless an exception is approved.
6. Retention periods must be documented by the data owner.
7. Data past its approved retention period must be securely deleted unless a legal hold applies.

## Roles
Data owners classify data; IT applies access controls; users follow handling rules.

## Exceptions
Exceptions require documented approval and an expiry date.

## Review
Review every six months.
